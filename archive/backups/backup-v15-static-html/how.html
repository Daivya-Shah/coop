How it works

Here’s how it works.

New York is full of little groups of people who make time for each other, and lovely neighborhood spots that could use a few more people on a quiet night. We thought, why not introduce them?

01 Tell us a little about yourself.

Have a club looking for somewhere to meet? Or a space that could use a little more company? We’d love to hear about it.

02 We’ll see who might get along.

We look for places and groups that make sense together: the right neighborhood, the right night, the right kind of crowd. Nothing gets booked until everyone’s happy with the plan.

03 Make yourself at home.

The first get-together is just the beginning. Hopefully, before long, it’s the spot everyone knows to show up to.

Restaurants, bars & cafés

Got a quiet night? We’ve got some ideas.

Maybe Mondays are a little slow. Maybe there’s a back room that doesn’t get used much. We find local clubs looking for somewhere to meet regularly and introduce them to places like yours.

Your space. Your rules.

  • You decide which nights work and how many people you’re comfortable hosting.
  • You set any spending expectations, and you always have the final say before anything is booked.
  • No fee to join the pilot, and your first completed matched event is free of any Coop fee.
  • After that, a small percentage for completed club nights, agreed together in advance.

Clubs, communities & little gatherings

Your people deserve a place.

Maybe your book club keeps hopping between cafés, or your run club needs somewhere to wind down. Tell us what you’re looking for and we’ll help you find a neighborhood spot you can come back to again and again.

No membership fee.

  • Coop is free for clubs to join.
  • No venue-hunting every week. Just a place for your people.
  • Some places may have a minimum food or drink spend; you’ll know the details before agreeing to anything.
  • Weekly, every other week or monthly: whatever works for your group.

01 / The ideaNot another app

Right now a wine bar in Flatiron is half-empty on a Tuesday. Twelve blocks away, a book club is squeezed into somebody’s living room again. Coop introduces them — then keeps the night going, week after week.

No cold DMs.

Organizers stop messaging fifteen bars and hoping one writes back.

No weekly scramble.

One standing night, one place, the details already sorted.

No empty Tuesdays.

Venues pick their slow nights. We fill them with people who come back.

p.s. We’re not a directory or a booking marketplace. We’re the people who find the fit, sort out the details, and send a recap after every night.

02 / Four stepsNobody’s committed until both sides say yes

How it works

  1. 01

    Venues tell us their quiet nights.

    Which days, how many seats, any spend minimum, house rules on noise and activities. Two minutes, no account.

  2. 02

    Clubs tell us how they really meet.

    Who actually shows up (not follower counts), which neighborhoods, how often, and what members are happy to spend.

  3. 03

    We propose a real group for a real night.

    A specific club on a specific evening. The venue approves, the club approves, terms go in writing. Either side can say no.

  4. 04

    It turns into a habit.

    Reminders, headcount updates two days out, a recap after each night, and the next date locked in before anyone leaves.

03 / Two sides, one tableWhat each side gets

Two sides of the same table.

For restaurants, bars & cafés

Turn your quiet nights into regulars.

  • Fill the hours you pick as slow, with groups that come back weekly.
  • You set the dates, capacity, activities and any minimum spend. Nothing’s booked without your OK.
  • Headcounts ahead of time, and a heads-up the moment numbers change.
  • One person to deal with, not a dozen organizer DMs.
  • After each night: who came and what the group spent.

Good fit: an independent spot in a walkable neighborhood, room for 10–40, and weeknights that drag.

04 / The fine printIt’s short

Free to try.
Fair after that.

  1. Listing is free. So is applying, and so is talking to us.
  2. Your first matched night has no fee. If it’s not worth it, walk away.
  3. After that, pick one: 10% of the group’s verified food & drink (pre-tax), or a flat fee per night we agree on upfront. Never both.
  4. No fee on cancelled nights. And organizers never get stuck covering your losses.
  5. Clubs pay nothing. Not now, not as a surprise later.

Exact rate and how we count the group’s spend are agreed in writing before your first night.

05 / QuestionsThe ones people actually ask

Questions

Is this a booking app?+

No. There’s no marketplace to scroll. We find a group and a venue that fit, sort out the terms with both of you, and look after the night every week after that.

Who decides which group comes in?+

The venue does, every time. We send you a specific group — what they do, how many usually come, when, and what they tend to spend. You say yes or no.

What does it cost a venue?+

Nothing to list, and nothing for your first matched night. After that it’s either 10% of the group’s verified pre-tax food & drink or a flat per-night fee — you choose before your first night, and it’s in writing.

Do clubs or their members pay anything?+

No. Clubs join free and members don’t need to download or sign up for anything. People just buy their own food and drinks, like any night out.

What if fewer people show up than expected?+

We check numbers a week out, two days out and the day before, so nobody’s surprised. If turnout drops, we sort it early — a smaller area, adjusted terms if the venue agrees, or a different spot. Organizers are never on the hook for a venue’s losses unless that was agreed upfront.

How do you know what the group spent?+

Simply: a group tab, an event code, a reserved section, or the manager’s total for the night. No POS integrations. If a number is disputed, nothing gets invoiced until it’s sorted.

Where are you starting?+

Two pockets of Manhattan: Chelsea / Flatiron and the East Village / Lower East Side. Close enough that we can visit, find backup spots, and do it properly before going further.

Do you already have clubs and venues lined up?+

We’re signing up our first pilot venues and clubs right now. We’ll only introduce a group when there’s a real fit — we won’t promise demand that isn’t there yet.

Because every flock needs one.

We match social clubs with restaurants, bars and cafés that have empty seats on their slow nights. The club gets a table it can count on. The venue gets regulars.

How it works

  1. 01The venue

    A great spot that sits half-empty on its slow nights. Venues tell us which nights, how many seats, and any minimum.

  2. 02The clubs

    Knit, book, art, run and language clubs: plenty of people, no regular place to meet.

  3. 03The match

    We pair a club with a venue that fits on area, size and night. Both sides say yes before anything is booked.

  4. 04The regulars

    Same table, same club, every week. Reminders, headcounts, and a short recap after each night.

For venues

  • You pick the nights, the seats and any minimum spend.
  • Nothing's booked without your OK.
  • Headcounts ahead of time, and one person to talk to.
  • Free to list. Your first matched night has no fee.

For clubs

  • A place that fits your size, neighborhood and budget.
  • One standing night, no renegotiating every week.
  • Members don't need an app or an account.
  • Always free for clubs. Turn down any match.

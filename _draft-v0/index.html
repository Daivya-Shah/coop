How Coop works

Built like a local operating layer for recurring demand.

Preferred nights, capacity, spend requirements, and blackout dates all live in a simple venue intake flow.

02

Operating model

We are not a directory. We are a recurring-demand engine.

Pilot terms

Free listing. First completed matched event is paid only after approval.

After the pilot, venues choose either 10% of verified qualifying group F&B spend or a negotiated flat fee. No hidden fee stack.

Match quality

Hard constraints first, then human review.

We look at timing, capacity, geography, spend comfort, and venue policies before a proposal is sent.

Founder-led

Software only after the operating workflow is proven.

The first product is the minimal system needed to match, coordinate, and report on recurring community events responsibly.
